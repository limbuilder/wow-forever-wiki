Unofficial pre-Beta encyclopedia for **World of Warcraft: Forever** — races, racials, classes, talent calculators, dungeons, and Forever vs Classic diffs.

**Live site:** [https://wowforeverwiki.org](https://wowforeverwiki.org)

> Not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment. Content is compiled from public announcements and community sources ahead of Beta (Sep 2026) and may change.

## Overview

**World of Warcraft: Forever, documented.** Races, racials, nine classes, talent calculators, dungeons, and Forever vs Classic diffs — labeled by source confidence for the pre-Beta window.

Browse hubs for races and classes, open per-class talent calculators, compare Forever vs Classic, and read the official Deep Dive systems guide. The UI is bilingual (English / 中文) with client-side wiki search.

Community talent reference: [Forever Talents](https://forevertalents.up.railway.app/)
